ACTIONS
DRIVE / FERRY
Move to an adjacent city.
COMMERCIAL FLIGHT Reveal an Allied city card in your hand to move to the matching city. Discard a Neutral City card to move to the matching city. You cannot move to a Soviet city using the Commercial Flight action.
UNRECORDED FLIGHT
Discard the City card that matches your city to move to any city.
NEUTRALIZE AGENT
Remove an agent from your city.
SHARE INTEL Either: give a card that matches the city you are in to another player or take that card from another player. The other player must also be in the city with you.
BUILD SAFEHOUSE
Discard the City card that matches your city to build a safehouse there.
IDENTIFY TARGET CITY While at a safehouse, discard 3 City cards matching your region to reveal the target city of your region under an Objective card.
ASSEMBLE TEAM While at a safehouse, discard 5 City cards matching the affiliation of your city to put a team of that affiliation there.
DRIVE / FERRY TEAM
Move a team to an adjacent city.
ACQUIRE TARGETS Select an Acquire Target objective. If you have active teams in the required cities, you complete it. If not, you fail that objective.
