DESTROY the Infection card of the city where you quarantined the population. Remove (or cross out) the Exposed City sticker near that city. It is no longer an Exposed City.

Flavor: The teams quarantined the infected population of the city to contain the problem. If a cure is found, those quarantined should be easy to treat.
