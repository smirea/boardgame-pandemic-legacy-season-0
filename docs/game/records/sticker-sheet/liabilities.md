LIABILITIES
If you scratch off a circle on your alias and reveal a %, add a Liability sticker to your character.
If necessary, write the name of the region where you received the liability.
FOLLOWED: If you end your turn in a Soviet city, without an agent, add an agent to that city.
AUTHORITIES WATCHING: You may not do the Neutralize Agent action while in a city with an Incident token.
HASSLED: If you end your turn in a Soviet city without a safe house, discard 1 card (after the Draw Cards step).

REGION:
MONITORED: If you draw 2 Soviet City cards at the end of your turn, lose 1 cover.

REGION:
AIRPORTS ALERTED: You may not do the Unrecorded Flight action from a Soviet city.
RECOGNIZED: If you are in this region, lose a cover whenever an Incident token is added to it.
